## 15. Words for the body — a checklist for builders A and B

A word passes if it is **true** (the body really does it, today, in everyone), **present tense, second person,
kind, believable**, short (≤ 80 characters), and it thanks or trusts rather than commands. Examples of the *kind* of
sentence the research allows (not prescribed wording):

- "Każdej nocy Twoje komórki sprzątają i naprawiają." — the contract's own example; true in general terms (the body repairs, much of it in sleep), no promise.
- "Twój oddech zwalnia, serce się uspokaja." — true in a slow-breath rest (Laborde 2022).
- "Twoje ciało codziennie tworzy nowe komórki." — true (Sender & Milo 2021: ≈ 330 billion a day, mostly blood).
- "W spokoju Twoje ciało ma siłę na naprawę." — fair (stress slows repair; calm supports it), not "heals".
- Not: "Twoje komórki odmładzają się" · "Uzdrawiam się" · "Twój guz się zmniejsza" · "Aktywuję regenerację" ·
  "Twoje DNA się naprawia dzięki tej medytacji" · "Rosną Ci nowe zęby/włosy" · any "I" affirmation of a state the
  listener may not believe ("Jestem w pełni zdrowy") — gendered and unbelievable.

## 16. Notes for the lead on the contract's wording (inner.ts header)

- "slow breathing (6 a minute) raises vagal tone" — **yes** (Laborde 2022: vagally-mediated HRV).
- "believable: unbelievable affirmations backfire" — **yes, for people with low self-esteem** (Wood 2009); one study.
- "the oldest form of suggestion" — a historical claim, not checked; harmless in a code comment, keep it out of the
  app's words.
- "vivid imagery felt from inside works best" — the evidence is that **kinaesthetic** imagery engages the motor cortex
  and visual imagery alone did not (Stinear 2006, small); that it gives *more strength* is not directly shown. In the
  app: "imagery felt from inside" is fine; "works best" only as a gentle tip.
- "a word paired with deep calm brings calm back later" — **can help**; the cue's own effect is old and small
  (McGlynn 1978: placebo as good). Keep it modest.
- "most repair runs in deep sleep" — **too strong**: wounds heal day and night. True: deep sleep goes with growth
  hormone release (Van Cauter 2000), sleep supports immune defence and inflammatory balance (Besedovsky 2019), and in
  mice the brain clears waste faster in sleep (Xie 2013). Say "much of the night's work / sleep supports repair".

## What we may say (plainly, in the app's words)

1. **Stress slows the body's repair; calm supports it.** Wounds heal more slowly under stress (meta-analysis, r ≈ −0.4);
   relaxation and similar psychological help (most clearly in surgical patients) aided healing in several trials
   (promising, mixed, possible publication bias).
2. **Calm reaches the body.** Slow breathing (about 6 a minute) raises the heart's vagal rhythm —
   during the practice and after training. Regular relaxation/meditation lowers some inflammation markers (CRP),
   modestly.
3. **A repeated prayer slows the breath by itself** (the rosary at ≈ 6 breaths a minute) — a word on the out-breath is
   an old, gentle way to rest.
4. **A word paired with calm can help bring calm back** later (applied / cue-controlled relaxation) — an old,
   gentle technique; its own effect is modest and not separated from expectation, so say "can help", never more.
   For the app the deeper reason is prayer: a short prayer word said with the breath.
5. **Expectation is real.** What we expect changes what we feel — even an openly given placebo helps some symptoms.
   The prefrontal cortex carries the expectation: when it is briefly disrupted, placebo pain relief disappears.
6. **Imagined movement strengthens muscle** (moderately, less than real training) — and imagery **felt from inside**
   (the sense of the movement) engages the motor system; merely seeing it does less.
7. **How we see our own ageing goes with later health** (small, robust association) — so kind, true words about the
   body are better than fearful ones.
8. **Words work best when they are believable**, kind and in the second person ("You…"), not grand self-praise.
9. **Much of the body's upkeep is tied to sleep**: deep sleep goes with growth-hormone release; sleep supports immune defence and
   inflammatory balance; a rested face looks healthier. Bright screens late in the evening delay melatonin; a calm,
   absorbing picture before sleep helps some people fall asleep sooner.
10. **Every day the body makes new cells** (about 330 billion a day, mostly blood).
11. **The band sees a real part of the path, not the healing**: the prefrontal cortex is where calm, expectation and
    reappraisal are made, and (its medial part) helps steer the stress response and the heart's rhythm
    (Thayer 2012; McKlveen 2015 — rodent and human work).
12. **Relaxation helps people with cancer feel better for a while** (distress, comfort) — about the person, never
    about the tumour (Faller 2013, Roffe 2005).
13. **This supports — it never replaces — treatment.**

## What we must not say

- That focus, imagination, a word or the band **heals, cures, regenerates, rejuvenates, regrows** anything; that it
  "activates" healing, "programs the subconscious", "reverses ageing", "makes you younger".
- That **imagery shrinks tumours** or fights cancer; that a positive attitude decides the course of an illness (and so,
  by implication, that the ill are to blame). The evidence for imagery in cancer is for **comfort and anxiety**, not
  for the disease (Roffe 2005).
- That **hypnosis heals**; that affirmations **change DNA, genes or telomeres** (gene-expression and telomere findings are
  small, early, biomarkers — never a promise).
- That meditation **lengthens telomeres** or slows ageing (early, small, an outlier drives the effect).
- That positive emotions "build health" through vagal tone (Kok 2013 is disputed).
- That **open-label placebo or expectation works for everything** — it is for symptoms (pain, fatigue, IBS…), modest.
- That the band **measures healing**, inflammation, immunity or the body's repair — it measures prefrontal activity.
- That sleep "makes beautiful" or "rebuilds" — say rested, repaired, renewed in what the body truly does at night.
- Any number we have not checked here. No "energy field", "vibration", "manifest", "detox", "universe".
- Never a command to an organ ("heal!", "grow!"); never guilt ("if you focus enough…").
