# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-24

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Emmanuel — God with us. Neurofeedback that trains the stillness where prayer begins.
**What it does:** Emmanuel connects to a consumer brain headband — Mendi (fNIRS) or BrainAccess HALO (EEG) — right in the browser and turns its live signal into practice: a focus training modelled on Mendi's, a hemispheric-balance trainer, three training games (Geode, Lighthouse Keeper, Butterfly House) that reward calm, steady attention, and two Christian games (HolyHolyHoly 2D/3D). Everything runs on the device, offline, in Polish, English, German and Swedish.
**Product category:** Christian contemplative practice × neurofeedback ("prayerful neurofeedback"). Where people look: Christian meditation / prayer apps, meditation headband apps, Mendi / Muse companion apps, neurofeedback games.
**Product type:** Free, open-source (AGPL) progressive web app; installable; no account.
**Business model:** Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no loot boxes; voluntary donations may come later. Hardware is sold by its makers (links in the app, no affiliation, no commission).

## Target Audience
**Target users (B2C):** Christians (Catholic, Orthodox, Protestant — ecumenical) who want a deeper, quieter prayer life and struggle with a restless mind; owners of a Mendi or HALO headband who want more than one training game; contemplatives and retreat-goers curious about what their attention is doing.
**Primary use case:** A short daily practice (3–10 min, ~3 times a week) that settles the mind before or as part of prayer, with honest feedback on calm and focus.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Help me become still enough to pray, instead of fighting my thoughts."
- "Make a daily attention practice something I look forward to."
- "Show me honestly whether I'm getting calmer and more focused over time."
**Use cases:**
- Settling down before evening prayer, the rosary, lectio divina or a reading.
- A focus warm-up before study or work, framed as stewardship of the mind.
- A family or youth group trying the Christian games (CBT skills, prayers of the heart).

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Attention is scattered; stillness — the ground of prayer and meditation — is hard to find and harder to keep.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Christian prayer apps (Hallow, Pray.com, Abide) guide words and audio but give no feedback on the state of the mind.
- Neurofeedback apps (Mendi, Muse) are secular, single-mechanic, and often subscription-based.
- Meditation apps (Headspace, Calm) carry a secular or Eastern-spirituality frame some Christians are uneasy with.
**What it costs them:** dry, rushed prayer; guilt; abandoned routines; an expensive headband gathering dust.
**Emotional tension:** "I want to be present to God, but my mind won't stop." Suspicion that technology and faith don't mix.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Mendi app — one game, secular, focused on productivity; Muse — meditation soundscapes, secular.
**Secondary:** Hallow / Pray.com / Abide — rich Christian content, no biofeedback.
**Indirect:** silence, retreats, the rosary, a spiritual director — irreplaceable; Emmanuel is a practice aid next to them, never instead.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- The only app that joins Christian contemplative framing with live neurofeedback from consumer headbands.
- Honest training: adaptive thresholds, rest blocks as well as focus blocks, nothing lost to a bad signal, real data shown plainly.
- Games designed from neurofeedback research and today's best mobile game loops.
- Private by design: no account, no cloud; free and open source; four languages.
**How we do it differently:** We train attention and stillness; we don't claim to produce faith or spiritual experiences. God's presence is a gift ("Emmanuel"), not an output.
**Why that's better:** It respects both the science and the faith, so believers can trust it and skeptics can't fault it.
**Why customers choose us:** meaning + feedback + play, at no cost.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Is this New Age / 'hacking' God?" | No. Emmanuel trains attention and calm, the way breathing or silence does. God is already with us; the app only helps you notice. Ecumenical, scripture-grounded. |
| "Does neurofeedback even work?" | Evidence is mixed and we say so. You see your own honest data, and the practice itself — stillness, steady attention — is worthwhile either way. |
| "My data?" | Nothing leaves the device. No account, no cloud, open source. |
| "I don't have a headband." | Try it with the simulator; the Christian games work without a band; the app shows where to buy one (no commission). |

**Anti-persona:** people seeking diagnosis or therapy (Emmanuel is not a medical device); people expecting guaranteed mystical experiences; strictly secular users who reject any faith framing (the training games stay neutral, but the brand is not).

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** distraction in prayer; boring, repetitive neurofeedback apps; subscription fatigue.
**Pull:** "God with us" — a tool that fits their faith; games they enjoy; honest progress; free.
**Habit:** existing prayer routines and apps; the Mendi app on their phone.
**Anxiety:** Is this theologically sound? Does it need an expensive headband? Is my brain data safe?

## Customer Language
**How the founder describes the aim (verbatim, translated):** "Connect the divine God, Creator of all that is, with the human — by changing the state of consciousness with techniques common to meditative states."
**How we say it publicly:** "Train the stillness where prayer begins." "God is already with us; Emmanuel helps you notice."
**Words to use:** stillness, presence, attention, calm, prayer, practice, gift, listen, quiet, steady, honest.
**Words to avoid:** "neurohacking", "hack", "unlock the divine", "altered states", "manifest", "energy/vibrations", "guaranteed", "cure", "therapy", "medical"; never "Gott mit uns" in German (Wehrmacht belt motto) — use „Gott ist mit uns“; prefer „Gud är med oss“ in Swedish.
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Emmanuel | Hebrew ʿImmanu-El, "God with us" (Isaiah 7:14, Matthew 1:23) |
| fNIRS | light-based measurement of prefrontal blood oxygenation (Mendi) |
| EEG | electrical brain waves (BrainAccess HALO) |
| Focus / Calm blocks | push and rest phases of a training round |

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** quiet, warm, reverent without being churchy; honest; light in the games.
**Style:** short sentences, concrete words, second person, no slogans or exclamation marks in the faith copy.
**Personality:** contemplative, trustworthy, gentle, intelligent, quietly playful.
**Visual identity:** a "night instrument" — deep ink, warm off-white text, one accent colour; the wordmark `Emmanu<span>el</span>` lights the Hebrew "El" (God); the icon is a neuron whose synapse holds a small gold star — the place where human attention meets the light.
